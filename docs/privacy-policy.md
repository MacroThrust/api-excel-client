# Privacy Policy — MT Data Connector

**Effective date:** 16 June 2026  
**Provider:** MacroThrust  
**Application:** MT Data Connector (Excel Office Add-in)

---

## What this policy covers

This policy applies to **MT Data Connector**, the Excel add-in published by MacroThrust.

---

## Information we collect

We collect only the account details you agree to share when you sign in with your Microsoft account (for example, your name and email address, as shown on the sign-in screen).

We do not collect any other personal information through the add-in.

---

## How we use it

We use your account details only to sign you in and operate the add-in.

---

## Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Sign-in is handled by Microsoft; we receive only the account details you approve during sign-in.

---

## Deletion

You may request deletion of your account details at any time by emailing [mattange@gmail.com](mailto:mattange@gmail.com). You can also sign out in the add-in (**MT Menu → Sign Out**) and remove the add-in from Excel.
